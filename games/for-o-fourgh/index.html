In this world, you either follow god O’Foughr or you’re against him.

Gain influence ⚜️ and collect gold 💰 and relics 🏺. If you have less than 0 influence ⚜️, you’re out! Click the buttons (of number keys) to travel and choose tasks to earn gold 💰, relics 🏺, and influence ⚜️. Each character has a different win goal. Each game allows 5 players, but you can also play against up to 5 bots.

Saint: Total pray count > 15

Baal: 7 sins have been committed

Marx: Saint, Devotee or Baal has zero influence

Devotee: Saint has 10 💰 + 🏺 + ⚜️

Dissident: Total money > 20
